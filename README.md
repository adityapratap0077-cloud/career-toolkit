## What this is

The job-search and career skills I use for applications, outreach, and study-abroad prep. Small list, both earn their place. No code copied here, only links to the originals plus notes.

## What's inside

| Tool | What it does | Link | License | My note |
|------|--------------|------|---------|---------|
| ai-job-search | Applications, CV tailoring, interview prep, master's SOPs | [MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search) | check upstream | My copy is adapted for my own use. The fit evaluation and SOP help are the strong parts. |
| career-ops | Job portal scanning, application tracking, outreach | [andrew-shwetzer/career-ops](https://github.com/andrew-shwetzer/career-ops) | check upstream | Also adapted. Good for keeping applications organized instead of in your head. |

## How I use these

ai-job-search for tailoring the CV and prepping, career-ops for tracking where everything stands. Together they cover the whole pipeline from "saw a posting" to "got the reply".

## How to use this list

Pick the tool that matches your problem, follow its link, install it per its own README. Star the ones you keep coming back to.
